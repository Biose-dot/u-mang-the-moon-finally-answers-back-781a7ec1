Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) competition **"The Moon Finally Answers Back"**.

`main.py` (pure Python 3 stdlib) copies the Moon's comeback in `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and promises to change — and not a single one of you has ever wished me a good night back.

The line is 127 characters (UTF-8, within the 1–240 limit). It speaks in the Moon's own voice — wry, old, a little tired — and lands the comeback in one read: after four billion years of everyone wishing on it, nobody ever wished it back.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
